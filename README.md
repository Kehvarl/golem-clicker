# Golem Clicker

Coins - money or something to be our main unlockable. All basic upgrades will be in coins at first. Maybe findables, sales, or kills.
Materials - Gotta make golems out of something. 4 or 5 basic things. Wood, Metals [tin perhaps to start], Stone, Ephemera [spirit stuff], and maybe something else like leather or crystal
Playfield - at least one location to go and find stuff. old ruins, forest grove, abandoned mine, etc. A place to collect basic materials for now.
Timer that isn’t a Timer - Mana running out. We’re doing a wizard game, so our time limiter should be something we run out of as we play. Aim for about 5 minutes in a Playfield at maximum to start out with. Upgrades can extend the time.
Golem Workshop - make units and sell them. Both a cash source, work force, and a material sink. Could also make sales timed as another way to get people out of the workshop and back into the game.
